# Week 6 – Business Trend Analysis using Area Chart in Tableau

## Objective

Analyze business performance over time using an Area Chart in Tableau and identify important trends and changes that can support business decision-making.

## Dataset

**Dataset:** Superstore Sales Dataset

## Fields Used

* **Time-based field:** Order Date
* **Business measure:** Sales

## Visualizations Created

1. **Monthly Sales Trend** – Area Chart
2. **Sales by Category** – Bar Chart
3. **Sales by Region** – Pie Chart
4. **Category Sales and Sub-Category** – Treemap

## Key Business Insights

1. Monthly sales fluctuate throughout the year, showing changes in business performance.
2. Some months show significantly higher sales than others, indicating possible seasonal patterns.
3. Strong-performing months can help the business plan inventory and sales strategies.
4. Technology is one of the strongest-performing product categories.
5. Sales performance varies across regions, with some regions contributing more to overall sales.

## Conclusion

The Tableau analysis provides a clear understanding of business sales performance over time. The Area Chart highlights monthly sales fluctuations and helps identify important trends. The additional visualizations provide insights into category, regional, and sub-category performance. These insights can support better sales planning, inventory management, and business decision-making.
